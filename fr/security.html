What you can rely on.

Your data stays yours

Your documents, emails and knowledge remain your property. We make no claim to them.

Swiss by default

Zuraio runs in Switzerland. Other AI models are labelled, and only that task's content goes to them, when you choose.

Access follows your rules

Zuraio uses your existing Microsoft or Google users and groups. People only see what they're already allowed to see.

Nothing goes out without your OK

Zuraio prepares replies and changes as drafts. A person checks and sends.

A clear record
