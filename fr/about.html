À propos de Zuraio

Une IA pratique. Conçue autour des personnes.

Why we started

Built from the same problem our clients face.

We needed a better way to prepare for meetings, manage emails, find the right information, draft reports and reduce repeated admin. Public AI tools were useful, but they were not enough for serious company work.

We wanted one controlled layer connected to real business knowledge, with clear rules around access, data and model choice. That is what became Zuraio AI Hub.

What we believe

  • Human judgement comes first.
  • Company data stays under company control.
  • Start with real workflows.
  • Make sources and access clear.
  • Build tools people can use every day.
