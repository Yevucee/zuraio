One platform, seven layers.

One platform, seven layers.
1. Access People work in the Zuraio interface or in connected apps.
2. Identity and permissions Microsoft or Google identities and groups, plus optional Zuraio roles. Checked before any knowledge or action is used.
3. Orchestration Understands the task, picks the workflow, and coordinates knowledge, tools and models.
4. Skills and company knowledge Versioned skills with templates and quality rules, managed outside the AI model.
5. Assistants and integrations Specialised assistants, connected via MCP, APIs, webhooks and connectors.
6. Model gateway Chooses an approved model per task, based on data class, location, quality and cost.
7. Records and operations Requests, sources, actions and approvals are logged according to the agreed audit set-up.

From request to result.

  1. 1A person asks a question or starts a task.
  2. 2Zuraio checks who they are and what they may access.
  3. 3The right skill supplies the steps, templates and rules.
  4. 4Zuraio picks the assistants, sources and an approved model.
  5. 5It prepares an answer, a draft or a proposed action.
  6. 6Anything that changes data or goes out waits for a person's OK.
  7. 7Sources, versions and approvals are recorded.

How skills are kept under control.

New or changed skills go through review before anyone uses them. Older versions are kept, so you can always see what applied when.

What's recorded.

Every answer leaves a trail your IT partner can check.

The request: who asked what, and when.

The sources: which documents and systems were used.

The skill: which skill and version applied.

The result: what Zuraio produced.

The approval: who checked and released it.
