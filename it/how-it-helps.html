Come aiuta Zuraio

Meno amministrazione. Più lavoro di valore.

Zuraio aiuta i collaboratori a preparare, cercare, redigere, riassumere e fare follow-up utilizzando la conoscenza aziendale approvata. Lavora accanto ai sistemi esistenti e riunisce il contesto pertinente quando serve.

Use cases

Where Zuraio helps day to day.

Practical workflows for meetings, email, reports, knowledge search, admin and process consistency — not abstract AI experiments.

01

Meeting preparation

Generate a briefing before a meeting using relevant emails, documents, client history, open tasks and previous notes.

Examples:

  • client meeting brief
  • supplier negotiation brief
  • internal management meeting brief
  • project status briefing
  • board preparation notes
02

Email and follow-up

Summarise long threads, draft replies and detect open actions.

Examples:

  • What did the client ask for?
  • What do we still owe them?
  • Draft a reply in our tone.
  • Create a follow-up reminder.
03

Reports and summaries

Draft updates from approved documents and internal sources.

Examples:

  • monthly management summaries
  • project updates
  • sales account reports
  • internal decision notes
  • board briefing packs
04

Internal knowledge search

Ask questions across documents, policies, standards, project folders and other approved knowledge sources.

Examples:

  • What do we usually include in this proposal?
  • Where is the latest version of this policy?
  • What did we agree on this project?
  • Which document supports this claim?
05

Admin and task capture

Turn emails, notes or voice inputs into structured tasks, reminders and follow-ups.

Examples:

  • meeting actions
  • client follow-up tasks
  • internal reminders
  • admin checklists
  • project handover notes
06

Process consistency

Help teams follow the same steps and reduce avoidable human error.

Examples:

  • proposal checklists
  • client onboarding
  • report templates
  • compliance document reviews
  • recurring admin workflows

Augmenting people

AI that supports your people — not replaces them.

Zuraio is designed around a simple principle: AI should support human judgement, not replace it. The goal is to reduce repeated preparation, surface relevant context and help teams work faster — while keeping people in control of decisions, approvals and final outputs.

People stay accountable

Outputs are designed to be reviewed, edited and approved by the people responsible for the work. Zuraio assists with drafts and summaries; your team decides what is sent, shared or acted on.

Sources you can check

Where internal knowledge is used, answers can include references to the documents or systems they came from. This is intended to make it easier to verify information before acting on it.

Workflows that fit how you work

Rather than asking employees to change how they work entirely, Zuraio is designed to connect to existing tools and knowledge — meeting preparation, email, reports and search in the places teams already spend their time.
