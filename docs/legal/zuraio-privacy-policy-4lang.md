# Zuraio privacy policy: draft for legal review (DE, EN, FR, IT)

Status: draft. Not legal advice. Based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP / revDSG, in force since 1 September 2023). Everything in [square brackets] must be confirmed by the team before publication. The go-live guard blocks the production sync while brackets remain.

## What the team must confirm before the lawyer reads it

1. Legal name, address and contact for data protection questions (same as Impressum).
2. Where the **website** is hosted for zuraio.ch (provider and country). If it's GitHub Pages, data goes to the USA and section 6 must say so.
3. Where **contact form** messages go (the endpoint api.zuraio.ch: which server, which country, which email provider receives them).
4. Which **email provider** the founders use (e.g. Infomaniak, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace) and its country.
5. Whether the site uses **analytics** (there is an analytics script in the code: which tool, or none) and whether any **cookies** beyond technically necessary ones are set.
6. Whether **fonts** are loaded from Google Fonts or served from our own server.
7. How long contact enquiries are kept (the draft says 2 years after the last contact).
8. Whether Zuraio also targets customers in the **EU**. If yes, the lawyer should add a GDPR section (legal bases, EU representative if needed).
