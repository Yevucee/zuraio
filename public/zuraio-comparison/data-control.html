Data control

Keep control of company data while using AI.

Zuraio is designed for companies that want AI benefits without uncontrolled data exposure, unclear model routing or generic external processing.

Why it matters

Company knowledge is more than information.

Company knowledge includes client context, pricing, negotiations, internal decisions, project history, employee knowledge and commercial judgement. Once that knowledge enters uncontrolled tools, companies may lose visibility over where it goes and how it is used.

Data ownership

Your data stays under your control.

Zuraio is designed to process company information within the boundaries of the agreed deployment and contract. Ownership, permitted use, retention and processing terms must be documented for the selected solution and model providers.

Company retains ownership

Your documents, e-mails, business records and internal knowledge remain your property. Zuraio does not claim ownership of customer content.

Model-provider terms are defined before connection

Whether information may be retained or used by an external model provider depends on the selected model, provider and contractual terms. These conditions must be defined before company information is connected.

Identity and permissions

Access should follow existing company rules.

Zuraio can work with established Microsoft or Google identities, groups and permissions. Where those integrations are not used, Zuraio can provide additional platform roles and access controls.

Existing identity structures

Microsoft or Google identity information can provide the organisational basis for user, group and role access.

Permission checks

Identity and permission context can be passed to the relevant assistant and data source so access can be checked when information is retrieved or an action is requested.

Additional Zuraio controls

Zuraio policies can add restrictions for models, tools, data classes and actions without replacing the company’s underlying identity system.

The exact permission behaviour depends on the connected system, integration method and agreed deployment.

Controlled actions

Reading information and changing information are not the same.

Zuraio is designed to distinguish between read-only work and actions that change documents, systems or external communication. Critical or writing actions can be prepared as drafts or proposals and held for confirmation.

  • Read approved information within the user’s permissions
  • Prepare a proposed response or change
  • Show the intended action before execution
  • Require confirmation where configured
  • Retain relevant action and approval information where supported

Draft-first behaviour is implemented in EmailAI. Broader action controls depend on the component and deployment maturity.

Model and location policies

Control which data may go to which model and location.

Policies can take account of the task, data classification, permitted processing location and approved model provider. This supports architectures that combine local, Swiss-hosted and approved cloud components.

Security

Security depends on the selected deployment.

Zuraio deployments are designed to protect data in transit and at rest. The exact encryption, key management, storage, logging, backup and recovery arrangements must be documented for the selected components and deployment.

Authentication and authorisation

Access is controlled through identity, roles, scopes and component-specific permissions.

Tenant and data separation

The required isolation model is defined for the selected deployment and components.

Logging and review

Relevant requests, sources and actions can be retained according to the agreed audit model and the maturity of each component.

Deployment-specific safeguards

Network, storage, backup and operational controls are agreed as part of the deployment design.

Specific statements about Swiss data-protection law, GDPR, AI regulation, certifications and subprocessors require separate legal and technical verification.
