Knowledge

Turn scattered company information into governed, usable context.

Important knowledge is often spread across inboxes, shared drives, meeting notes and business systems. Zuraio brings approved information and working methods together so employees and specialised assistants can use them more effectively.

SkillOS

From stored information to governed company knowledge.

Zuraio’s SkillOS manages approved knowledge and process instructions as versioned, reusable skills. A skill can define the information to use, the process to follow, the responsible roles, the permitted tools and the expected result.

This means Zuraio does more than search documents. It can provide a specialised assistant with the approved knowledge and working method required for a particular company task.

Versioned knowledge

Skills have defined versions and lifecycle states, so approved guidance can be reviewed and updated outside the AI model.

Connected context

Retrieval can combine keywords, structured metadata, semantic similarity and relationships between related skills.

Governed changes

New or changed skills can pass through review and approval before being made available for operational use.

Skills

Company knowledge includes how work should be done.

A Zuraio skill can contain more than reference material. It can describe the approved process and the conditions under which it should be used.

  • Required process steps
  • Roles and responsibilities
  • Inputs, dependencies and expected results
  • Templates and formatting requirements
  • Quality checks and review points
  • Permitted sources, assistants and tools
  • Version, validity and replacement of earlier guidance
Approved sources

Approved sources for company-specific answers.

The connected sources depend on the organisation, permissions and agreed integration scope. Zuraio is designed to use selected company information rather than relying only on public model knowledge.

Documents and files

Policies, standards, proposals, project files, templates and approved shared folders.

E-mail and calendar

Relevant communication, meeting context and correspondence where access permissions and integration status allow.

Business systems

CRM, ERP, project and specialist systems that contain operational context.

Process knowledge

Released SkillOS skills containing approved procedures, templates and quality rules.

Custom sources

Additional information made available through approved APIs or customer-specific connectors.

The exact sources and integration status are confirmed for each customer setup.

Traceability

Answers that can be checked against their sources.

Where source-backed company knowledge is used, Zuraio can retain and present source information according to the selected workflow and interface. This helps employees verify important information before using it.

  • Check important claims against original information
  • Understand which documents or systems contributed
  • Reduce reliance on unsupported model memory
  • Support internal review and governance
  • Identify the relevant version or date where available

Source presentation differs by assistant, interface and deployment. Not every output is necessarily source-backed.
