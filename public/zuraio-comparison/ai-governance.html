AI governance

AI use that can be understood and reviewed.

Responsible company AI requires more than a model. Organisations need clear rules for knowledge, access, models, actions, human review and operational records.

Governance

Governance begins before an answer is generated.

Zuraio can apply rules to the sources, skills, specialised assistants, models and actions used for a task. Approved process knowledge can be versioned in SkillOS, while critical changes or external actions can be prepared for human confirmation.

Approved knowledge

Released versions of company skills and guidance can be made available for operational use.

Defined access

Identity, role and source permissions determine which information and capabilities may be used.

Controlled execution

Tool inputs can be validated, and read-only work can be treated differently from actions that change information or communicate externally.

Human confirmation

Sensitive or writing actions can be held as drafts or proposals where the relevant component supports this workflow.

Relevant records

Requests, sources, versions, actions and approvals can be retained where supported by the selected component and deployment.

Skill lifecycle

Keep company guidance current and controlled.

SkillOS manages knowledge and process skills through a defined lifecycle. Proposed changes can be reviewed before a new version becomes available for operational use.

Draft → In review → Approved → Released → Deprecated → Archived
  • Quality checks before status changes
  • Separate proposal and approval steps
  • Protection of released skills from uncontrolled edits
  • Version history and snapshots
  • Escalation when quality issues are detected
Draft-first

AI can prepare. People remain responsible.

EmailAI implements a draft-first principle for actions that change information or communicate externally. The system can prepare a response or proposed action, show the intended result and wait for confirmation where required.

The available approval and action controls vary by component. Full Office document-change workflows remain specified or in development.

Limitations

Important limitations.

AI-generated results may contain errors, omissions or outdated information. Important information should be checked against original sources before it is used.
